# Product icons

Line icons that picture products on the shopping list, one SVG per key.
`manifest.json` names every icon in German and English, the store section
it belongs to and where the drawing comes from:

- `tabler`: taken from [Tabler Icons](https://tabler.io/icons), MIT License,
  Copyright (c) 2020-2026 Paweł Kuna
- `lucide`: taken from [Lucide](https://lucide.dev), ISC License,
  Copyright (c) Lucide Icons and Contributors
- `own`: drawn for this project and covered by its license

Icons named `section-*` are the generic fallback of a store section, shown
for products that have no icon of their own. The iOS app carries a copy of
this directory.
